The Black Door Society is for the part before that. The covers that lose. The scenes that disappear. The research rabbit holes. The ideas that look brilliant at midnight and considerably less brilliant the next morning. You get to see the work while it is still alive.

The finished book is only half the story.

I write horror fiction, but The Black Door Society is built around everything readers normally never get to see: the wrong covers, the wrong turns, the research that becomes unexpectedly important, the scenes that vanish, and the moment a story finally starts behaving like itself.

I don't want a community built around asking people to “support a creator.” I want a room worth entering.
