A place for the stories before and after publication

I don't want to build an author site that feels like a catalog.

I want this to feel like the room beside the book—the place where you find the abandoned ideas, early art, research, readings, strange details, and eventually the conversations that happen while the next story is still becoming itself.

Book One · The first release

The Names
We Buried

Some names disappear because they were forgotten. Others disappear because someone needed them buried.

The Names We Buried is the first story through the Black Door: a horror novel circling memory, guilt, silence, and the dangerous things people agree never to say aloud.

The finished novel will define the details. For now, this is the promise at its center: something was put away, people learned to live around the absence, and the past has decided that being forgotten is no longer acceptable.

What gets buried doesn't always stay dead. Sometimes it only loses its name.

About the author

Jeff writes horror with the door left slightly open.

I like horror that begins somewhere recognizable and then changes one small thing until the whole place feels wrong.

This site is where I'm building that work in public without turning the writing process into a performance. The novels remain the center of it. Everything around them—the art, conversations, readings, scraps, wrong turns, and eventually the Black Door Society—is here for readers who want to get closer to how those stories are made.

For now, I'm beginning with The Names We Buried. The rest are waiting their turn.
